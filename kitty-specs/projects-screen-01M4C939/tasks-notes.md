## Two-parent joins

WP04 needs WP02 and WP03; WP08 needs WP06 and WP07. The plan says the tool gives a lane one parent, but on 4.0.0rc5 `finalize-tasks` accepted both declared dependencies and `lanes.json` records nine lanes with lane-d depending on lane-b and lane-c, and lane-h on lane-f and lane-g (verified first-hand at finalize, no collapse). The allocator should therefore merge both dependency lanes at claim; if it refuses or conflicts, the orchestrator merges the second parent (`git merge --no-ff`, `custom-elements.json` resolved by regeneration only, status files by the standing rule). Each join WP's first stop-check verifies both ancestries.
